Privacy

Watch Together keeps two video players in step: when one person presses play, pause or skips, the other person's video does the same.

What is sent

  • A room code you create or type in.
  • Whether the video is playing or paused, its position in seconds and a timestamp.

What is not sent

  • No names, emails, accounts or passwords.
  • No page addresses, site names, video titles or browsing history.
  • No video, audio or screenshots.

Storage

The server keeps only the latest play/pause position of each room, in memory. Nothing is written to disk, and it disappears when the server restarts. The add-on stores your room code and timing adjustment in your own browser.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. There are no ads and no analytics.

Why the add-on can run on all sites

It needs to find the video player on whatever site you watch on. It only reads and controls the video's play, pause and position.
