## Store listing
**Name:** Watch Together

**Summary (max 132 chars):**
Watch movies with someone far away. Press play on your side - it plays on theirs. Works with the video player on any site.

**Description:**
Watching a movie with someone in another city and counting "3, 2, 1, go"? Watch Together keeps both videos in step for you.

How it works:
1. Both of you open the same movie or episode - each on your own site and account.
2. One person starts a room and sends the short code. The other joins.
3. Play, pause or skip on either side - the other video does the same, within a fraction of a second.

- Works with the video player on almost any site, even if you are on two different sites.
- Keeps you in step automatically: a video that falls behind quietly catches up.
- Different intros or Bluetooth delay? Nudge your side by 0.1 s until it matches.
- No accounts, no sign-up, no ads.

**Category:** Entertainment
**Language:** English

## Privacy tab
**Single purpose:** Keep video playback (play, pause, position) in sync between people in the same room.

**Permission justifications:**
- *storage:* remembers your room code and your timing adjustment.
- *Host permission (all sites):* the add-on has to find the video player on whatever site the user watches on, so it can play, pause and seek it in step with the room. It only reads and controls the video element's play state and position.

**Remote code:** No, I am not using remote code.

**Data usage:** tick nothing (no personal data, no browsing history, no website content is collected). Certify the three statements.
